⛏ How it works

Good choices give pickaxe swings. Tap a rock next to a tunnel to dig. Glints show what’s inside. Tap a gem you found to send it up to your temple.
Bad choices prop your newest tunnel with wood, hay or stubble. At the fire those props burn and the tunnel caves in, burying everything beyond it.
