## Keeping chores at the heart of it

As the farm grows, selling eggs, wool, milk, apples and honey can bring in as many coins as the jobs. Two rules keep the jobs essential (Joel's choice, 18 Oct 2026):

- **Your animals work as hard as you do.** Animals only lay, grow wool, give milk, and the garden, orchard and bees only produce, on days the child finished a routine (all morning jobs, or all after-dinner jobs) or did at least 2 extra jobs. On other days the animals eat and babies grow, but nothing is made ("😴 Your animals are resting today").
- **⭐ Job stars.** Every job done gives 1 star (morning, after-dinner, bedtime and confirmed extra jobs). Stars never come from selling. Big things cost stars as well as coins, about 1 star per 10 coins:

|---|---|---|---|---|

Mystery eggs, mystery boxes, fence repairs, feed, shears and milk bottles need no stars, so the everyday loop never stalls. A child doing about 8–10 jobs a day reaches the big farmhouse's stars in roughly the same 3–4 months as its coins, so neither farm income nor jobs alone can rush it.

**Selling (halved 24 Nov 2026):** eggs 8 · duck eggs 10 · golden eggs 20 · 🌈 rainbow eggs 50 · wool 15 · milk 10 · apples 3 · honey 10 · truffles 8. In the shop, a grown animal can be sold: hen 40, lizard 75, snake 75. The 🏪 farm stand adds 1 coin for every 5.

**Rules for animals:**
- Animals never die. Hungry animals just stop making things until they're fed.
- Space is limited until the big barn: 8 small animals, 2 sheep, 1 cow. After the barn: 14, 4 and 3. A grown hen can be sold for 40 to make room. Dinosaurs: 1 (2 with the barn).
- A new farm starts with a free mystery egg, 5 free feeds and a fence that lasts 7 days.

**The fence and the fox:**
- The fence lasts 7 days (14 with the strong fence), and fixing it costs 10. When 2 days or fewer are left, the app says "Fix your fence!" out loud.
- A fox only comes once the fence has been broken for a whole day. It has a 35% chance each day, takes one grown chicken or duck (never babies) and leaves small flocks of 3 or fewer alone. It takes at most one per absence. A grown crocodile keeps foxes away.

**Mystery box (45):** 35% a feed bag (5 free feeds) · 25% a mystery egg · 15% golden corn (a baby grows up instantly) · 12% a lamb · 10% a decoration · 3% a calf.

## New friends: Aussie Island Mates and Rock Pool Crew (Nov 2026)

Twelve named sticker characters keep the farm fresh from Big Farm (about week 4) through months 2–6. They are a **coin and star sink, never a source**: they make nothing to sell, eat free with the one "Feed everyone" tap, and sell back for well under what they cost.

| 🫧 Rock pool egg (needs the rock pool) | 150 + ⭐15 each | Myrtle the sea turtle, Rocket the squid, Puff the pufferfish, Inky the octopus | 🐄 Dream Farm |
| 🛶 Jetty (needs the rock pool) | 450 + ⭐45 | Zoom the manta ray | 🐄 Dream Farm |

All twelve cost **2,700 coins and 270 stars**, about a month of stars on top of the farmhouse and big farmhouse, so they fill months 2–6 without speeding anything up.

- **Kid-true:** the egg-layers really hatch from eggs and the app reads the fact aloud ("Platypuses lay eggs! A baby platypus is called a puggle."). Zoom comes by the jetty because manta ray babies are born, not hatched.
- **One of each.** Sea friends only live in the rock pool and swim there on the field. Eggs always hatch someone new; once all have hatched the egg shows "All hatched ✓".
- **No income.** Only perk: Digby sometimes digs up a decoration (no coin value). Tapping any friend plays their sound and reads their personality or fact.
- **Selling:** same pick → goodbye → hold → "Bring it back" steps. Prices: platypus, echidna, kookaburra 60 · squid, turtle, puffer, octopus 70 · wombat 80 · crab 80 · koala 100 · kangaroos 120 · manta 150. If a home's friend is sold, the shop offers "Invite back" for the same price they sold for (no stars), so selling and inviting back never makes coins.
- **Moods:** each picture looks hungry until fed today, sleepy once the night window opens (after 6pm), and happy otherwise.
- **Goal bar:** at Big Farm, once the calf is bought, it suggests the next friend. At Dream Farm it suggests the barn, windmill and farmhouse first, then the rock pool, the big farmhouse, the other friends, then the dinosaur egg.

## Farm visits

Each child can open their farm **twice a day for 3 minutes**, with their own code, once their current routine is done. A grown-up can open any farm (or the mine) from the Grown-ups portal any time, with no time limit.

## The Mine (gems, separate from coins)

| Pickaxe | Swings per rock | Gem rock / fallen-in rubble | Cost | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron (start) | 5 | 3 | — | — |
| Golden | 3 | 2 | 8 gold, 25 stone | 1–3 weeks |
| Diamond | 1 | 1 | 30 gold, 3 amethyst, 3 sapphire, 3 emerald, 3 pearls, 150 stone | 2–3½ months |

**Treasure limit:** 2 a day to start, and 1 more with each pickaxe upgrade. Gold earned by finishing a routine or praying doesn't count toward it.

**Sorry moments make the newest tunnel squares fall in:**
- Owning up: nothing falls in, unless it was a big one (rated 4–5), which costs 1 square.
- Getting caught: 2 squares, or 3 for a big one.
- Squares with a wobbly prop that still needs making right never fall in.

**The mine never runs out:** it starts 40 rows deep and adds 10 more rows whenever digging gets near the bottom.

At one good moment for every sorry one, a child still moves forward.
